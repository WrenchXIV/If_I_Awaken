WHAT YOU JUST READ

…is the opening of If I Awaken in Los Angeles.

A 90-minute immersive theatrical experience that walks 150 guests through seven built environments — a tour of the real LA, captured inside a 30,000 sq ft converted studio in the Arts District.

Opening June 2027. Designed to anchor the city through the FIFA World Cup, Super Bowl LXI, and the 2028 Olympics.

We're raising $8.97M. This is what we've already done, what we're building, and what you'd be a part of.

THE WALK

Seven neighborhoods, one continuous walk.

No seats until the finale. No proscenium until the proscenium earns it.

01

HOLLYWOOD BOULEVARD

A 110-foot LED wall. Dancers as tourists. The Walk of Fame underfoot. Then the wall pivots — and the tour breaks open.

02

BOYLE HEIGHTS

A tianguis with stone tables. A band stage where two poets meet across a generation.

03

THE ASIAN ARC

Chinatown · Little Tokyo · Koreatown · Historic Filipinotown · Thai Town. One rapper-poet walking the line as each town's stage lights in turn.

04

BLACK LA · A CENTURY

A church wall opens. Gospel choir, swing dance at Dunbar, a hip-hop cypher, a Leimert Park drum circle.

05

FOLK LA · LAUREL CANYON

A knock at a door becomes a living room. The band joins the audience on the floor for a sing-along.

06

THE STAGE

A classical proscenium. The only seated room. A finale that earns its formality.

07

THE REAL LA

Back through the corridor we entered — but this time the wall is the audience's own faces.
