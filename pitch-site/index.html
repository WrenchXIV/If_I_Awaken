AN IMMERSIVE THEATRE EXPERIENCE · LOS ANGELES · 2027

If I Awaken in Los Angeles.

A 90-minute immersive theatrical experience that walks guests through the real Los Angeles — captured inside a 30,000-sq-ft converted studio in the Arts District, next to the iconic 6th Street Bridge.

An LA recreated and reimagined. Guests are guided through built sets and live performances spanning every neighborhood and culture that makes the city what it is.

THE WALK

Seven neighborhoods, one continuous walk.

From the red carpet to the real LA. Use the dots below or arrow keys to walk the tour.

01

HOLLYWOOD BOULEVARD

A 110-foot LED wall. Dancers as tourists. The Walk of Fame underfoot. The wall pivots — and the tour breaks open.

02

BOYLE HEIGHTS

A tianguis with stone tables and a band stage where two poets meet across a generation.

03

THE ASIAN ARC

Chinatown · Little Tokyo · Koreatown · Historic Filipinotown · Thai Town. One rapper-poet walking the line.

04

BLACK LA · A CENTURY

A church wall opens — gospel choir, swing dance at Dunbar, a hip-hop cypher, a Leimert Park drum circle.

05

FOLK LA · LAUREL CANYON

A knock at a door becomes a living room. The band joins the audience on the floor for a sing-along.

06

THE STAGE

A classical proscenium. The only seated room. A finale that earns its formality.

07

THE REAL LA

Back through the corridor we entered — but this time the wall is made of the audience's own faces.

PHASE 1 · DURING RECOUPMENT
98% Investors 2% Venue

98% of net profit flows to the investor pool (pro-rata by capital) until the full $10.79M is returned. 2% to the venue.

PHASE 2 · POST-RECOUPMENT
50% Investors 48% Company 2% Venue

Ongoing: 50% investors / 48% company / 2% venue. Once the venue's 2% is capped (1.5–2× its rent discount), it reverts to the company and the split becomes 50 / 50.

VENUE ARRANGEMENT

The venue trades below-market rent for a 2% profit-share across both phases, capped at 1.5–2× the lifetime value of the rent discount (market rent − actual rent). After the cap, the venue's share reverts to the company.
