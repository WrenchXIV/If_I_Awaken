M-03
SECTION THREE · THE THESIS

Los Angeles is the largest entertainment market in the world without an iconic show about itself.

New York has Sleep No More (13 years, $300M+ NYC + Shanghai). Vegas has Cirque (multiple billion-dollar resident shows). Santa Fe has Meow Wolf ($300M+ franchise). LA — population 18 million in greater metro, 50M+ annual visitors, the largest entertainment industry in human history — has nothing that fits the slot.

The opportunity is not to compete with stadium attractions. It is to be the cultural front door visitors and locals walk through when they want LA itself, not just the LA-shaped backdrop.

If I Awaken in Los Angeles is built to fill that slot: a 90-minute walking immersive that places the audience inside the actual neighborhoods, the actual people, the actual music — at theatre scale, in the Arts District, opening in time for the World Cup.

  • 30,100 sq ft converted studio. Arts District. Next to the 6th Street Bridge.
  • 150 guests per performance. 12 performances per week.
  • 100 minutes end-to-end. Seven built environments, one continuous arc.
  • 14+ month run. June 2027 — August 2028 (with extension optionality through the Olympic close).
