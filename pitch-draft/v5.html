ARTIST VIEW

An iconic American city, without an iconic show about itself.

Los Angeles is the world's storyteller. It writes the films, scores the music, broadcasts the news, mints the celebrities. It is, in every measurable way, the city that tells the world's story.

And yet it has never told its own.

We are making the show LA has been missing — a 100-minute walking immersive that puts an audience inside the actual neighborhoods, with the actual people, with the actual music. At theatre scale. In a 30,000 sq ft warehouse in the Arts District.

ARTIST · THE PILOT

August 1, 2025. The Ford Theatre. The first read on whether the city wanted us.

Directed by Gina Belafonte. Composed by Derrick Hodge. On stage: Luis J. Rodriguez, Samantha Rios, Dante Basco, Mendeleyev.

The line stretched around the block. We held the curtain thirty minutes.

"They didn't just speak about LA — they spoke as LA." — The Hollywood Times

INVESTOR · PROOF POINT 1

Sold-out premiere. Oversold. Hard demand signal at venue scale.

The pilot was a controlled experiment in demand. In partnership with the LA Philharmonic. Verified attendance.

Pre-sales filled the house. Walk-up overflowed it. The 30-minute curtain hold is a clean data point: the room was capacity-constrained, not demand-constrained.

ARTIST · THE AWAKENING

Then the city wrote back.

We asked one question — "Tell us about your LA" — and inadvertently started the largest poetry competition in the country.

Partnered with the Los Angeles Unified School District and Get Lit — Words Ignite. Judged by Aloe Blacc, José Olivarez, Olivia Gatwood, Safia Elhillo, Chen Chen, and Gina Belafonte. Schools, classrooms, community centers, cafeterias. Voice answered voice.

This is owned audience. We can recontact every submitter, every teacher, every judge for opening night.

ARTIST · THE WALK

Seven neighborhoods, one continuous walk.

Hollywood Boulevard → Boyle Heights → the Asian Arc → Black LA → Folk LA → the Stage → the Real LA.

No seats until the finale. No proscenium until the proscenium earns it. A 110-foot LED wall that pivots into faces. A church wall that opens into a drum circle. A door that becomes a Laurel Canyon living room.

The audience does not watch a show. They take a walk.

INVESTOR · THE ASSET

A 30,000 sq ft venue with six monetizable assets in one building.

110-ft LED wall (LA's most dramatic indoor screen). 64-ft bar program. 150-seat proscenium theater. Outdoor red-carpet courtyard with marquee. 30,000 sq ft cocktail-cap floor. Arts District ZIP.

Most LA venues offer one or two of these. We have all six — which underwrites both the show AND a meaningful events business on dark days.

ARTIST · WHAT IT TAKES

ARTIST · THE FORM TRAVELS

If I Awaken in every city.

At its core, the form is honest: tell the truth about a place through the people who live there. That principle scales.

Chicago — house music, South Side to lakefront. New York — five boroughs, one breath. London — every postcode a different song. Each city, its own local cast, its own local truth, the same architecture underneath.

INVESTOR · THE FRANCHISE

LA is chapter one of a replicable IP model.

Production architecture (lighting, sound, projection, walk-through staging) is the IP. Each new city = new local cast, new revenue line, lower marginal CapEx.

Comp: Meow Wolf $300M+ across Santa Fe, Las Vegas, Denver, Houston, Grapevine. Sleep No More NYC + Shanghai + planned Shanghai 2.0.
