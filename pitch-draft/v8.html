Q02 · TIMING & PLACE

Why Los Angeles, and why now?

LA is the largest entertainment market in the world without an iconic show about itself. New York has Sleep No More. Santa Fe has Meow Wolf. Vegas has Cirque. LA — 18M metro, 50M+ annual visitors, the largest entertainment industry in human history — has nothing in the slot.

Now, because of three consecutive years of structural attention on Los Angeles:

  • FIFA World Cup '26. First major influx.
  • Super Bowl LXI '27. Mid-cycle.
  • Summer Olympics & Paralympics '28. 15M+ unique attendees, 2B+ media impressions.

We open June 2027 — with a full year of run-time before the Olympic surge arrives. The window is once-in-a-generation. Visitors will need a cultural front door that isn't a stadium.

Q12 · RISK

What are the real risks?

In order of how seriously we take them:

  • Build/permit timing. 30,000 sq ft of custom buildout on a fixed opening date is the biggest schedule risk. Mitigation: GM Mark Chinapen has run buildouts for Coachella, Stagecoach, the Grammys; opening date built with two months of contingency before Olympic surge.
  • Cast retention across an 18-month run. Immersive shows that grind down their performers underdeliver. Mitigation: union scale, swing cast, scoville-style rotational programming used on Here Lies Love.
  • Macro / consumer discretionary. Ticket price softness in a downturn. Mitigation: tiered pricing structure (Community $50) keeps demand floor high; events business adds a non-ticket revenue line.
  • Concept fit for a national/international audience. LA-specific story has to land for visitors. Mitigation: the universal frame ("a real city told by its real people") is built into the concept, and the pilot drew critical acclaim from non-LA names (Jodie Foster, Alex Hedison).

If you want the full risk register, ask — we'll send the DD pack.
