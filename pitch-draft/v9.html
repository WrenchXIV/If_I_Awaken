The Invitation

The premise. The city. The window. Three years the world will look at Los Angeles, and an immersive theatrical experience built for that moment.

I.1 THE PREMISE RAISE · $10M · OPEN · JUNE 2027

An iconic American city without an iconic show about itself.

Los Angeles is the largest entertainment market in the world without an iconic immersive show about itself. New York has Sleep No More. Vegas has Cirque. Santa Fe has Meow Wolf. LA — 18M metro, 50M+ annual visitors — has nothing in the slot.

If I Awaken in Los Angeles is the show built to fill it: a 90-minute walking immersive that puts 150 guests inside the actual neighborhoods, with the actual people, with the actual music. 30,000 sq ft in the Arts District. Opening June 2027.

I.2 WHY NOW 2026 · 2027 · 2028

The world is about to look at LA. Three years in a row.

FIFA World Cup '26. Super Bowl LXI '27. The 2028 Summer Olympics & Paralympics. Tens of millions of visitors. Tens of billions of media impressions.

We open June 2027 — a full year of run-time before the Olympic surge arrives. The window is structural, scheduled, and once-in-a-generation.
